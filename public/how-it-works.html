The map

One path in, one gate, one document out

The gate is the part that changes the business. Nothing reaches drafting until the compliance evidence is actually there.

Solid lines carry the client · the dotted line is the system learning

Stages 01 – 03

Before the gate

Everything here is something the platform does today, not a roadmap item.

01 Brad

The client is created

Brad adds a client and the platform mints a private onboarding link for that person alone. The record is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it touches disk.

Yields → encrypted client record + private discovery link

02 Sarah

Sarah runs the discovery session

The client opens the link on any device, at any hour. Sarah speaks to them, listens, and asks one plain-English question at a time until the picture is complete.

  • Ten sections: personal details, contact, family and dependants, employment and income, assets, liabilities, expenses, superannuation, insurance, and goals.
  • She gives no advice. This is discovery only, and she says so.
  • Rough answers are fine — Brad refines the detail at the meeting.

Yields → structured fact find, written straight into Brad's review screen

03 Beacon

The raw session becomes a working file

Beacon normalises whatever Sarah gathered, plus anything Brad typed himself, into adviser-ready sections. Completion is scored, and anything still missing is flagged in amber rather than quietly left blank.

Yields → completion percentage + named list of gaps

Stage 04 · Guardian

The interlock

This is the only place the flow forks, so it is the only place worth raising your voice.

Guardian scores the file from zero to one hundred against the seven best interests duty steps, the six safe harbour steps, Charter's AFSL 234665 obligations and the current ATO thresholds. It returns blockers, warnings and missing information by name.

Track A — held

SOA generation stays locked. The panel lists exactly what is missing, so there is no guessing. Cipher chases the client, and the file loops back to Beacon when the answer lands.

Returns to stage 03

Track B — cleared

Score of sixty or above, no blockers, and Brad's sign-off recorded. Only then does the gate open and drafting become possible.

Stages 05 – 11

Past the gate

From a cleared file to a document in the client's hands.

05 Scribe

Brad is briefed before he walks in

Scribe turns the cleared file into a meeting brief: what matters to this client, what to confirm, and what to ask. Afterwards it turns the conversation into adviser notes.

Yields → meeting brief, then post-meeting notes

06 Brad

The meeting, and the strategy call

Brad meets the client and approves the strategies himself, from the built-in set, the ATO and MLC catalogue, or ones he writes. The machine never chooses the strategy.

Yields → approved strategy list for this client

07 Orion

The evidence packet is assembled

Orion gathers the approved facts, the compliance notes, the meeting context and the projection inputs into one packet — and flags anything still absent. It is explicitly barred from drafting advice.

Yields → evidence packet + missing-input report

08 ATLAS

The strategy is written in Brad's voice

ATLAS synthesises the packet into tailored recommendations: reasoning, alternatives considered and rejected, expected outcomes, and the assumptions behind every projection. It writes to Brad's voice rules — no jargon, no markdown, plain human language.

Every strategic output is stamped adviser consideration only, requires Brad review.

Yields → drafted strategy sections, flagged for review

09 Platform

The document assembles itself

Twelve sections come together with the projection table, a market snapshot captured at the moment of generation, and a final compliance re-validation. Brad watches it build stage by stage.

Yields → complete draft SOA with its own compliance reference

10 Brad

Review, edit, approve

Every section is editable in place, with comments, reviewed and approved checkboxes, a compliance ring and flagged items. Nothing goes out unless Brad ticks it.

Each edit is recorded as a before-and-after pair. The next draft lands closer to how Brad would have written it himself.

Yields → approved SOA + a system that has learned something

11 Client

The client receives the document

A branded PDF: full-bleed navy cover, running header on every page, contents, all twelve sections, projections, market context and disclosures. Alongside it sit the fact find export, pre-filled MLC, AIA and AMP MyNorth forms, and the compliance certificate.

Yields → the finished advice, and the paper trail behind it

What actually changes

The shift, in Brad's terms

Not faster typing. A different shape of practice.

Before

Discovery happens in Brad's diary, one client at a time, in an hour he could have spent advising.

Now

Discovery happens on the client's phone, on their evening, without Brad in the room. He arrives to a completed file.

Before

Compliance is a review after the advice is written — so a gap means rewriting the document.

Now

Compliance is a gate before drafting. A gap is caught while it is still a phone call, not a rewrite.

Before

The same client details get re-entered into the fact find, the provider forms and the SOA.

Now

Entered once. One record drives the fact find, the forms, the certificate and the SOA.

Before

Every SOA is drafted from scratch, and sounds like whoever drafted it.

Now

Every SOA is drafted in Brad's voice, and every edit he makes teaches the next one.

Before

Proving best interests duty means reconstructing the reasoning later, from memory and file notes.

Now

Each SOA carries its own compliance reference and audit trail, recorded as the advice was built.

Underneath all of it

The part that has to be right

Encrypted at rest

Every client record is sealed with AES-256-GCM. Without the key the platform refuses to save client data at all — it never writes plain personal information.

Locked to Brad

Adviser sign-in with authenticator-app two-factor and lockout on repeated failures. Every adviser page sits behind it.

A real audit trail

Compliance events are logged durably and encrypted, so the record of what was checked, and when, survives the conversation.

Client privacy up front

Clients see an Australian Privacy Principle 5 collection notice before the session starts, and reach it by private token rather than a login.

Australian data residency

The platform is set up against a Sydney-region database, with the encryption key held by Brad in a password manager he controls.

Brad stays the adviser

Every strategy output is marked adviser consideration only. Nothing reaches a client without Brad ticking approved.
